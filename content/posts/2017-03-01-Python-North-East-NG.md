This month I officially take the reins of the Python North East user group. Myself and
[Scott Walton](https://twitter.com/scott_walton) are taking over from [Rowan
Hargreaves](https://twitter.com/rowanharg) and [Kieran
Darcy's](https://twitter.com/KieranFDarcy) fine stewardship.

I'm looking forward to the opportunity to meet many Python people and encourage new
members to try the best programming language there is!

We are in the process of refreshing the website and have set up a new [chat
channel](https://gitter.im/pythonnortheast/PublicChat?utm_source=share-link&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=share-link)
on Gitter. If you want to get in touch the best way is either on Gitter or via our Twitter
account [@pythonnortheast](https://twitter.com/PythonNorthEast).
